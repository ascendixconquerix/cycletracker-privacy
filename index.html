Period Tracker: Cycle Diary — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026 · Effective immediately

Data controller: Ascendix Conquerix — contact sarahhhaa0102@gmail.com.

Period Tracker: Cycle Diary ("the App", package com.ascendixconquerix.cycletracker) is developed by Ascendix Conquerix. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App handles and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. It does not require an account, never asks for your name, email, phone number, or location, and does not integrate any advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDK.

The App does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send any data from your device.

2. Locally Stored Data

The information you enter — period start and end dates, cycle settings, daily diary entries (flow, symptoms, mood, notes), reminder settings, language preference and your PIN (stored only as a salted hash) — is stored only on your device using Android app preferences. This health-related data is never uploaded by us, is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, and is removed when you use Settings → Delete all data in the App, clear its data in Android Settings, or uninstall the App.

3. Permissions

  • Notifications — used only for optional reminders before your next period and before ovulation. Notification text on the lock screen is kept generic. You can decline or disable this at any time in the App or in system settings.
  • Run at startup (receive boot completed) — used only to restore your scheduled reminders after the device restarts.

The App does not access your location, camera, microphone, contacts, files, or the network.

4. Advertising

The App contains no advertising.

5. Your Rights (EEA / UK)

Because the App stores your data only on your device and we never receive it, you can access, correct, or delete everything yourself by using the App, by using Delete all data, or by clearing its data or uninstalling it. If you have any question about your data, contact us at sarahhhaa0102@gmail.com; you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 16 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

7. Health Disclaimer

Predictions of periods, fertile days and ovulation are estimates based on the dates you enter. The App is not a method of contraception, is not a medical device, and does not provide medical advice or diagnosis. If your cycle changes or you have health concerns, consult a qualified healthcare professional.

8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and post the new version at this URL.

9. Contact
